Import Fitbit data

A web page can't sign in to Google Health directly, so bring your data in from an export.

  1. Request your Fitbit data from Google Takeout (takeout.google.com, choose Fitbit).
  2. Pick the zip here, or unzip it and pick the files you want: steps, distance, calories, exercise, sleep, resting heart rate, Active Zone Minutes.
  3. Any CSV with a date column plus steps, distance, calories, sleep or workouts also works.
